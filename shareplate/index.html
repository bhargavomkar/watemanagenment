From surplus to shared meals

How SharePlate works

A simple handoff connects food businesses with nearby people and community groups. Every step is visible in the offer and account activity.

01 · List

Restaurants post what is left

Create a restaurant account, then list the food, number of meals, price per meal, neighborhood, pickup date and time window. Choose a club partner and cutoff time; unclaimed food switches to a free donation for that club.
02 · Reserve

People or clubs claim an offer

Browse and search current offers. Sign in as a community member or club and reserve a meal bundle. The offer is removed from the available list so two groups cannot claim the same bundle.
03 · Collect

Meet at the listed pickup time

The reservation appears under My activity with the restaurant, location and pickup window. The receiver gets a one-time pickup code. The restaurant verifies that code at handoff.
Food Score

Recognize reliable contributions

Each restaurant starts at 50 points. A restaurant earns 10 points when it confirms a completed pickup. The score updates on its offers and account activity, making consistent food sharing easy to recognize.
